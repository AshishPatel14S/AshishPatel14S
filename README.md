## Projects

### [Airline Operations Intelligence](https://github.com/AshishPatel14S/airline-ops-intelligence)
`PostgreSQL` `SQL` `Power BI` `DAX` `Star Schema`

End-to-end analytics platform on **8.4 million BTS domestic flight records** across 17 U.S. carriers (2022–2023). Modeled raw data into a star schema (fact_flights + 3 dimension tables), wrote SQL with CTEs, window functions, and CASE-based delay attribution, and delivered a two-page Power BI executive dashboard covering on-time performance, delay root-cause breakdown, and carrier benchmarking.

Key finding: **76.5% of all delay minutes are operationally controllable** — carrier issues (37%) and late-aircraft propagation (39.4%) dwarf weather and NAS combined. The gap between best and worst carrier OTP is 16.9 percentage points.
